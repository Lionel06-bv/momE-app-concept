# momE: Parenting Companion App (Concept and Prototype)

A mobile app concept that brings childcare into one place: parenting knowledge, a growth logbook, reminders, a doctor consultation feature, a parent support forum, and child monitoring. Designed for the Creativity and Innovation course at BINUS University, aligned with UN Sustainable Development Goals 3 (Good Health and Well-Being) and 4 (Quality Education).

This is a design and business case study, not a coded application. The deliverable was a Figma prototype plus supporting research (customer jobs, pains and gains, value proposition canvas, and a cost and revenue model), summarized below.

## Problem and goal

Parents carry a long list of recurring jobs: learning parenting techniques, educating and raising their child, keeping up with medical checkups, finding fast and accurate solutions to a child's problems, recording a child's growth, and keeping the child safe. In practice this gets messy: appointments get forgotten, growth tracking is done informally or not at all, and reliable parenting information is scattered and hard to verify. momE addresses this by pulling curated articles, a growth logbook, reminders, a forum, monitoring, and doctor access into a single app, so parenting is easier, better documented, and less isolating.

## Target users

Personas developed for this project centered on parents at different stages of raising a child, each facing a mix of the pains above: needing trustworthy parenting information, struggling to keep track of appointments and milestones, and wanting reassurance that their child is safe even when not physically present with them.

## Features

1. **Parenting articles**: curated content reviewed by professionals
2. **Growth logbook**: records weight, height, motor skills, and milestones, alongside feeding, sleep, diaper, and medical or vaccination notes
3. **Child needs checklist**: daily tracking for eating, drinking, sleep, and development
4. **Parent forum**: a space for parents to ask questions and share experiences
5. **Reminders**: alerts for immunization, doctor visits, feeding, and other recurring schedules
6. **Child monitoring**: baby monitor integration with live camera feed, temperature and sound readings, and location tracking
7. **Online doctor consultation**: browse and contact doctors or therapists directly in the app

## Business model

- **Value proposition**: reliable, professionally reviewed parenting information, appointment and care reminders, a full growth record in one place, a supportive parent community, and remote child monitoring
- **Revenue streams**: non-intrusive advertisements on articles, plus a monthly subscription (Rp 15.000/bulan) for added benefits
- **Cost structure**: roughly split between recurring costs (hosting, custom domain) and one-time or in-house costs (development, development tools, Google Play developer fee)
- **Breakeven model**: projected to recover its first-year capital of Rp 4.000.000 and turn a profit with as few as 30 subscribed users in year one
